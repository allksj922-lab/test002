# Anti-AI Design Skill Guide (Bespoke Human-Crafted Aesthetic)

This skill overrides default AI design tendencies (often called "AI Slop") and produces web UIs that feel designed by a world-class human product designer.

## 1. What to NEVER Do (AI Slop Anti-Patterns)
- ❌ **NO Generic Purple/Indigo Gradients**: Do not use `bg-gradient-to-r from-purple-500 to-indigo-600` or neon glowing borders.
- ❌ **NO Pure Pitch Black (#000000)**: Avoid dead black backgrounds. Use rich, tinted neutrals like Obsidian (`#090a0f`), Zinc-950 (`#09090b`), or Warm Slate.
- ❌ **NO Endless Nested Cards**: Avoid placing cards inside cards inside cards with 10 different shades of gray.
- ❌ **NO Uniform 3-Column Repetition**: Vary the rhythm. Mix wide hero cards, compact list rows, and prominent data badges.
- ❌ **NO Generic Glassmorphism & Over-Blur**: Avoid bloated backdrop blurs (`backdrop-blur-2xl bg-white/5`) that look like 2021 template themes.

## 2. What to ALWAYS Do (Craftsmanship & Editorial Aesthetic)
- ✅ **Authentic Theme & Persona**: Tailor the UI to the domain. For a **Running Crew**, take inspiration from **Tracksmith, Strava, and Nike Running**:
  - Energetic athletic accents: **Electric Lime (`#ccff00`)** or **Vibrant Ember (`#ff5722`)**.
  - Solid, tactile borders: `border-zinc-800` or subtle 1px divider lines.
  - High-precision telemetry metrics: Distance (km), Pace (min/km), Elevation, Attendance counts formatted with tabular figures.
- ✅ **Typography Hierarchy**:
  - Strong, confident headings with tight tracking (`tracking-tight font-extrabold`).
  - Clear metadata labels with uppercase micro-copy (`text-[11px] font-bold tracking-wider text-zinc-400 uppercase`).
- ✅ **Crisp Interactive States**:
  - Tactile buttons with subtle hover lift and micro-shadows.
  - Active status indicators (pulsing dot for upcoming live runs, crisp badges for member roles).
- ✅ **Context-Aware Empty States & Roles**:
  - Clear, human tone for pending approval states.
  - Dedicated admin control surface that feels like a real mission-control board.
