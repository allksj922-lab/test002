# PACE CRU DESIGN SYSTEM (Editorial Athletic)

## Concept
A bespoke editorial web application for urban running crews, inspired by Tracksmith, Strava, and modern boutique athletic clubs.

## Color Palette
- **Background**: Zinc-950 (`#09090b`) & Deep Neutral Charcoal (`#121316`)
- **Surface / Card**: Zinc-900/90 (`#18181b`) with 1px border `border-zinc-800`
- **Primary Accent**: Electric Volt / Acid Lime (`#ccff00` / `lime-400`), high-energy athletic contrast.
- **Secondary Accent**: Safety Orange / Blaze (`#f97316`), for urgency, dates, and alerts.
- **Text**:
  - Primary: Pure White (`#ffffff`) or Zinc-100
  - Secondary: Zinc-400
  - Tertiary / Micro: Zinc-500
- **Status Badges**:
  - Pending: Amber-500/15 + text-amber-400
  - Approved: Emerald-500/15 + text-emerald-400
  - Admin: Lime-500/15 + text-lime-400
  - Rejected: Rose-500/15 + text-rose-400

## Typography
- Headings: Bold, tight letter-spacing (`tracking-tight font-black font-sans`)
- Metrics / Stats: Monospace or tabular figures (`font-mono font-semibold`)
- Badges: `text-xs font-semibold tracking-wide uppercase px-2.5 py-1 rounded-full`
